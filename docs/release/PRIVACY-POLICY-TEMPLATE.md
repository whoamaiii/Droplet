[Your name or company] ("we") makes Droplet, a macOS app that saves videos
from links you copy. This policy explains what information is involved.

## What we collect

**Nothing through the app.** Droplet has no analytics, tracking, advertising
or crash reporting, and we do not receive your video links, downloaded files,
browsing history or browser cookies.

## Purchases and license keys

Purchases are handled by **Lemon Squeezy**, our merchant of record. They
process your name, email address, payment and tax details under their own
[privacy policy](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/privacy). We receive the order
details Lemon Squeezy shares with sellers (such as your name, email and
order) to provide support and refunds.

When you activate, check or deactivate a license, Droplet sends **only your
license key** and a generic activation name ("Droplet for Mac") to Lemon
Squeezy's license service. A license check happens at most once a day.

## Downloading videos

When you download a video, Droplet's bundled download engine (yt-dlp)
connects **directly from your Mac to the video site**. Those sites can see
your IP address and requests as they normally would. If you choose
**Use browser login** for a download, the engine reads that site's cookies
from the browser profile you select, on your Mac, for that one download.
They are sent only to that video site, never to us.

## Engine updates

When you press the update button, the download engine downloads its newest
version from the yt-dlp project on GitHub. GitHub sees your IP address.

## Stored on your Mac

Your save-folder choice, the number of free downloads used, and your license
key (in the macOS Keychain). Deleting Droplet and its folder in
`~/Library/Application Support/Droplet` removes them; the license key can be
removed with **Deactivate this Mac** in Settings.
