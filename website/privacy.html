Downloading videos happens on your Mac

When you download a video, Droplet’s download engine (yt-dlp) connects directly from your Mac to the video site. That site can see your IP address and requests, as it does when you visit it. We do not receive your video links, the files you save, your browsing history or your cookies. Droplet has no analytics, tracking, advertising or crash reporting.

If you choose Use browser login for a download, the engine reads that site’s cookies from the browser profile you select, on your Mac, for that one download. They are sent only to that video site, never to us or to the license service. Droplet does not remember the browser you chose.

Engine updates

When you press the update button, the download engine fetches its newest version from the yt-dlp project on GitHub. GitHub sees your IP address.

Buying a license

Purchases are handled by Lemon Squeezy, our reseller and merchant of record. They process your name, email address, payment and tax details under their own privacy policy. We receive the order details Lemon Squeezy shares with sellers (such as your name, email address and order) and use them only to provide your license, support and refunds.

License checks

When you activate, check or deactivate a license, Droplet sends only your license key and a generic activation name (“Droplet for Mac”) to Lemon Squeezy’s license service. A license check happens at most once a day. Droplet never sends video links or cookies to the license service.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking, and loads nothing from other companies. It is hosted by {{host}}, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and operation. The price chooser and the demo run entirely in your browser.

Stored on your Mac

Your save-folder choice, the number of free downloads used and, if you have one, your license key (in the macOS Keychain). Deleting Droplet and the folder ~/Library/Application Support/Droplet removes them; the license key is removed with Deactivate this Mac in Droplet’s settings.
