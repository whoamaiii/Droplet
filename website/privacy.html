Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

{{owner}} (“we”) makes Droplet, a Mac app that saves videos from links you copy. This page explains what information is involved when you use Droplet and this website.

What we collect

Nothing through the app. Droplet has no analytics, tracking, advertising or crash reporting. We do not receive your video links, downloaded files, browsing history or browser cookies.

Purchases and license keys

Purchases are handled by Lemon Squeezy, our reseller and merchant of record. They process your name, email address, payment and tax details under their own privacy policy. We receive the order details Lemon Squeezy shares with sellers (such as your name, email address and order) and use them only to provide your license, support and refunds.

When you activate, check or deactivate a license, Droplet sends only your license key and a generic activation name (“Droplet for Mac”) to Lemon Squeezy's license service. A license check happens at most once a day.

Downloading videos

When you download a video, Droplet's download engine (yt-dlp) connects directly from your Mac to the video site. That site can see your IP address and requests, as it does when you visit it. If you choose Use browser login for a download, the engine reads that site's cookies from the browser profile you select, on your Mac, for that one download. They are sent only to that video site, never to us.

Engine updates

When you press the update button, the download engine downloads its newest version from the yt-dlp project on GitHub. GitHub sees your IP address.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted by {{host}}, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and operation.

Stored on your Mac

Your save-folder choice, the number of free downloads used, and your license key (in the macOS Keychain). Deleting Droplet and the folder ~/Library/Application Support/Droplet removes them; the license key is removed with Deactivate this Mac in Droplet's settings.

You can ask us which personal data we hold about you (in practice, only purchase and support records), and ask us to correct or delete it. Write to us at the address below. If you live in the EU, the EEA or the UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority.
